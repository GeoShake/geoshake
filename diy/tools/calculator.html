STA/LTA parameter calculator

Enter the sample rate your board actually measured — the firmware prints it every second as rate=.

Not the datasheet number. The real one, from the serial output.

ConstantValueWhat it is
alphaHp—high-pass coefficient, 2π·fc/fs at fc = 0.2 Hz
nSta—short window — 0.5 s of samples
nLta—long window — 30 s of samples
warmupSamples—equal to nLta; no triggers before this
warm-up time—how long until the detector arms
Paste into stalta.h 
—
Changing these breaks the golden vectors. The self-test values are locked to the reference constants (fs ≈ 109 Hz). Recalculate only if your rate is genuinely far off — a few percent shifts the windows by a few percent, which is harmless, and keeping the defaults means you can still prove your build matches the reference.
